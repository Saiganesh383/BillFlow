A SMALLER TO-DO LIST

Receipts to records.
In three simple steps.

  1. Upload an image

    Choose a clear JPG, PNG, or WebP of a bill or payment screenshot. You can upload several at once.

  2. Review the details

    On-device OCR reads printed English text. Check the suggested fields; low-quality images may need manual corrections. Dates and times are recorded as shown, without timezone conversion.

  3. Save and stay organized

    Search, edit, and export your payments. Records and receipt images stay in this browser on this device. Export a CSV backup before clearing browser data. CSV contains payment details, not receipt images.
